Suitability Override Process

When a client wishes to purchase a product above their risk profile, the following process applies:

Gap vs. Client RatingSituationRequired Action
Product = Client Rating✅ Fully suitableProceed normally
Product = Rating + 1⚠️ Slightly above profileClient acknowledgment of higher risk; RM notes on file
Product = Rating + 2🚫 Above profileFormal suitability waiver; Senior RM or Compliance sign-off; additional risk disclosures
Product > Rating + 2🛑 Significantly above profileCannot proceed without full re-profiling OR professional investor assessment
